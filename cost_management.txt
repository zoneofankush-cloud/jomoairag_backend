Cost Management & Financial Architecture
==================================================

1. COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) / Direct Operational Costs
(Scales directly with users - Tracked for Unit Economics)
--------------------------------------------------
- LLM API Cost: OpenAI/Gemini (Input/Output tokens)
- Search API Cost: Tavily API requests per query
- SMS/Communication: OTPs via SMS (Twilio, MSG91)
- Cloud Infra (Compute): Oracle Cloud VM charges
- Database & Storage: Supabase (PostgreSQL scaling) & Cloudflare R2
- Bandwidth & Requests: Cloudflare Pages/Workers requests
- Payment Gateway Fees: Stripe/Razorpay (2-3% per transaction)

2. OPEX (Operating Expenses) / Fixed & Variable Overheads
(Day-to-day business expenses in P&L)
--------------------------------------------------
- Payroll & HR: Founder Salary / Employee Salaries
- Office & Infra: Office rent, Electricity, Internet/Broadband
- Software Subscriptions: GitHub Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Cursor/Copilot, UI Tools (Canva/Figma), Accounting Software
- Domain & SSL: Yearly renewals (.com, .ai)
- Marketing & Sales: Ads, SEO tools, Email marketing
- Miscellaneous: Bank charges, Refunds, Chargebacks

3. Legal, Compliance & Audit (Professional Fees)
(To keep the company legally compliant)
--------------------------------------------------
- Setup / Advisory: Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, NDAs
- Compliance Retainers: CA/CS monthly/quarterly fees (TDS/GST)
- Audit Fees: Annual Report, Balance sheet signing, Statutory Audit
- ROC / MCA Filings: Annual Ministry of Corporate Affairs fees

4. CAPEX (Capital Expenditure) & Depreciation
(Balance Sheet Assets)
--------------------------------------------------
- Incorporation Cost: One-time company setup (Amortized)
- Equipment & Hardware: Laptops, servers, office furniture
- Depreciation: Yearly value reduction of hardware (Non-cash expense)

5. Taxes & Provisions
(Calculated on Net Profits)
--------------------------------------------------
- Corporate Income Tax: Tax on net profit
- GST (Goods & Services Tax): 18% Output liability minus Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- TDS: Withholding tax on employee salaries and professional fees
